GTA V PS3 Shutting Down

If you aren't already aware, GTA V is shutting down on December 16th, 2021.
Because of this, Paradise SPRX will no longer be sold or supported officially after December 16th.
Starting immediately, no more feature updates will be released for paradise.
Customer support will still be going and if any issues happen to arrise that require an update, one may be released in that case.

To any new buyers:
Paradise is currently on sale for 50% off with no refunds, and the price will drop futher and further until late November, where paradise will be free.
If you want to purchase paradise now and still support me in the last days, I appreciate that, but during the last few weeks before the shutdown I will make paradise free to everyone.

Additional info:
In the event that rockstar changes their mind (unlikely but some are hopeful!), the free keys will be deleted and paradise will continue to be sold for very cheap or maybe even free.
Assuming the gta end runs its course, the paradise website will continue to stay up until it has to be renewed and maybe I will renew it for another year.
